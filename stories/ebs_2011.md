##### The Interview

Graduating with a BSc in Computer Science in 2011, industry experience was virtually non-existent — university exposure was limited to C++ and a brief PHP course from 2009. A professor's recommendation opened the door to E-Business Solutions, and the interview began with a series of VB questions that exposed every gap in that knowledge.

The final question caught me off guard — I got it wrong. But I didn't stop there. I looked at the interviewer and said: **"Programming is a concept, not a language."** He paused, looked up with visible admiration, and I was in.

##### Thrown in the Deep End

I joined in July 2011 as an intern, starting with a week of Omni-Channel introductory videos — a steep, dry on-ramp into a system I barely understood. When my first ASP Classic task was finally assigned, I had no mentor. The team was busy; it was sink or swim.

The task took me four days. They said it was too long. My response was simple: _I'll do better, but I need some support_. They noted it. I moved on.

##### Finding Footing

The second ASP Classic task went significantly faster. Soon after, I was brought into a **native iOS tablet application** built in C#. It took about a month to become a genuinely productive member of the team — not bad for someone who had never written a line of C# or touched iOS development before joining.

##### The Breakthrough

While working on the iOS project, I became aware of a known problem that had been quietly shelved: an **RTL (right-to-left) slider control** that could not display or scroll correctly in Arabic layout. It was considered above my level and hadn't been assigned to me.

I decided to take it on anyway. After experimenting, I solved it using **dual-axis rotation** — a technique I worked out independently. It worked and _It put me on the map!_ From that point, harder tasks started coming my way and I kept rising to meet them.

##### Cut Short

Just as the momentum was building, my military enlistment came through. My time at E-Business Solutions ended in early 2012 — but the foundation it gave me in ASP Classic, C#, and iOS development, and the mindset of solving problems above my pay grade, stayed with me throughout everything that followed.
