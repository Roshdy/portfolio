---
name: CV & Story Narrative Tone
description: Tone guidelines for writing experience summaries, bullets, and story chapters
type: feedback
---

Keep all narrative — summaries, bullets, story chapters — professionally positive and forward-looking. Never frame situations as conflicts with leadership, management, or stakeholders.

**Why:** User wants to showcase proactivity and leadership without sounding confrontational, resentful, or politically charged. Specific phrase flagged: "against stakeholder resistance" — too combative.

**How to apply:**
- Replace conflict framing with initiative framing: "I advocated for X" not "I fought against Y"
- Replace resistance framing with adoption framing: "built a demo to accelerate buy-in" not "overcame opposition"
- Replace manager/leadership friction with organisational context: "within a conservative risk culture" or "navigating approval processes" not "my manager blocked it"
- When innovation was shelved, frame it as timing/risk appetite, not personal conflict
- The user's proactivity and results should speak for themselves without needing an antagonist
